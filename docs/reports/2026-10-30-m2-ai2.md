## Implemented behavior

Five existing mechanical skill levels and six existing personas are preserved:
Aggressor, Survivor, Duelist, Controller, Marksman, Flaky. Skills differ in
reaction, perception cone/range/memory, tracking/aim errors, turning/settling,
weapon switching and tactical thresholds. Personas remain orthogonal to those
mechanical settings and produce six distinct deterministic decision traces.
New optional native `bot_iSkillTier=1..5` and `bot_iPersonality=0..5` configure
any of 30 combinations independently before attach, preserving roster identity.
Defaults/invalid overrides retain the existing safe roster.

The reused single policy provides bounded LOS perception, frozen lost-target
memory, delayed acquisition, target threat/low-health/stickiness decisions,
weapon/ammo/range selection before aiming, existing projectile interception,
optional explicit observation-age lead, settling/shot discipline, pursuit,
strafe/damage reversal, health retreat, own-history escape, loss/search and
skill-dependent native respawn pulses. No hidden live-position tracking,
unmeasured RTT assumption, second target owner or extra respawn clock.

New M2 fixes close real integration gaps:

- Replicated death/absence/exclusion and pointer-generation change forget the
  opponent. The last aimed identity is also invalidated after memory expiration;
  unrelated empty slots do not reset aiming. Retained pointers are equality-only.
- Stop/map/rejoin reset invalidates the local player-source association on the
  timer thread, forcing fresh attachment even if an allocation address is reused.
- Native cadence commits only after accepted `SetAction`. Draw/current/wanted
  mismatch and recovery veto do not consume a shot interval. Wrong/stale/duplicate
  acknowledgements are ignored. Respawn fire never commits combat cadence.
- Empty usable mask cannot fire; knife/chainsaw approach 1.5 m and fire only <=2 m,
  across all 30 profiles. These are conservative policy reach assumptions.
- Recovery exclusively owns translation/yaw and clears combat strafe. Stationary
  human intent governs recovery hold instead of an unconditional 8 m hold.
- Reverse/sideways movement uses at most two existing wall rays at possible
  action-order headings. Clearance <1.2 m/nonfinite vetoes both axes. Existing final
  movement safety remains authoritative. This is not M3 navigation or safe-ground proof.
- Staged private startup disables early, configures all latched flags, then
  enables last. M2 smoke requires explicit P1 and positive strafe, <=2 bots / 300 s.

All experimental existing weapon2/lead2/strafe2/P1 switches still default OFF.
No original server/engine binary, hook, packet layout or synchronization threshold
was changed. Observation-age input remains 0 unless independently measured.

## Remaining acceptance gates and limitations

Full native gameplay acceptance is OPEN until evidence establishes the required
actual weapon/fire/hit/cadence, finite-ammo exhaustion/fallback, loss/reacquisition,
damage/retreat, death/respawn and profile-specific action behavior on this hash.
Short clean joins or telemetry alone do not prove these. Historical P1 four-bot
SYNCBAD remains unexplained; no root-cause fix or longer stability claim.

Inherited scope: damage awareness uses health loss, not armor-only impacts;
finite-ammo filtering does not account for infinite-ammo mode. Meaningful latency
calibration needs measured replication age. Local wall rays assume equal native
movement-speed scaling and do not establish swept collision, low obstacles,
momentum stopping or ground safety. Deferred minor: cached lateral value after a
wall veto can conservatively reject already-zero motion; emitted axes remain zero.
The independent reviewer declined to infer actual shots/hits from input ack and
confirmed pointer-equality scope, melee/no-ammo gates and recovery ownership.

Native gameplay gaps must remain visible in the handoff; no public release or
M1 acceptance follows from source/build completion. Next technical action is to
inspect actual short-run receipts/gameplay, address reproduced defects, and keep
unproven criteria NATIVE_QA_PENDING.
