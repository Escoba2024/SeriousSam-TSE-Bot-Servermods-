## Required integration behavior

1. Replicated opponent death/absence/replacement clears its perception and aim
identity; return requires a new reaction delay. Only pointer equality is used
for identity changes, never dereference a retained pointer.
2. Final native action emission owns P1 shot-cadence commitment. Weapon draw,
LOS/aim/navigation veto must not consume a shot interval. Direct portable policy
users retain their current immediate-commit default.
3. No usable weapon means no combat fire. Weapon2 selection precedes aim and
keeps ammo/blast checks authoritative.
4. Human movement intent governs obstacle recovery admission; proximity alone
must not suppress recovery during a requested retreat/chase. Final motion
safety continues to validate both axes and headings.
5. Verify all five tiers and six personas through behavior, alongside existing
reaction/aim/FOV/weapon/retreat/target-loss/reset tests and real adapter snippets.
